# MChair

MChair is a free appointment diary for mobile hairdressers, for Android. Visits out, clients at home and venues sit in one diary, with the travel between visits worked out, out days and home days, the day's route in Maps, mileage at HMRC's rates, call-out fees, client records with colour formulas, skin tests and photos, takings with a PDF report, widgets and password-locked backups. It has no adverts, no accounts and no paid version, and it cannot go online.
