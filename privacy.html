The short version

  • Everything you put into MChair is kept on your phone, encrypted. It is never uploaded, to the developer or to anyone else.
  • MChair does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything to a server.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. The developer receives nothing from your phone.
  • Copies leave the app only when you make them: a backup, a report, a spreadsheet, a client's record, a text or WhatsApp message, or the calendar copy you switch on.
  • You are in charge of your clients' details. MChair gives you the tools to look after them properly.

Who is responsible for what

MChair is a tool you use to run your business. The details you keep about your clients are held by you, on your phone, and the developer never has them. That makes you, the hairdresser, the one responsible for those details under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). The developer is not a processor of your clients' data, because none of it reaches him.

MChair helps you meet your duties. In Settings, under Data protection, you can give a client a copy of everything you hold about them, forget a client and all their records, find clients you have not seen for two, three or six years, and show or share a short privacy notice written for your clients.

What stays on your phone

MChair keeps its data in one file in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. The file is encrypted with AES-256 using a key held by Android's Keystore, so a copy taken off the phone cannot be read. Photos are kept as separate files, each encrypted the same way.

  • Your business: its name, your name, your home address and parking note, working days and hours, services and prices, call-out fee and mileage rates, and the person you send your day to, if you set one.
  • Your clients: the name, phone number, email, address, minutes and miles from your home, notes and alert you enter.
  • Bookings: the client, where it is (their home, yours or a venue), services, times, travel and miles, status, payments, call-outs, tips and reminders sent. Venues and journeys between clients that you set are remembered.
  • Care records: colour formulas, skin tests and their results, and before and after photos. Photos are re-saved inside MChair without the location and camera details the original may have held.
  • Time off, the waiting list and your settings, including the app lock. The PIN itself is not stored: only a salted, one-way hash of it.
  • If you set a backup password: a key made from it, kept inside the encrypted file, so daily backups can be made without asking you each time.

Skin test results and notes about a client's scalp or skin can be health information. Keep only what you need to look after the client safely.

Texts, WhatsApp and the calendar

When you send a reminder or an offer, MChair writes the message and hands it to your messaging app or WhatsApp, and you send it from there. If you switch automatic texts on, your phone sends tomorrow's reminders as ordinary texts at the time you set. Either way, the message travels like any other text or WhatsApp message you send, under your phone network's or WhatsApp's own terms.

On the Route screen you can send your day, with times, client names and addresses, to someone you choose. It opens as a text you see first, and goes only when you send it. Share it only with someone you trust.

If you switch the calendar copy on, bookings appear in the calendar you choose, with the address of each visit as its location. If that calendar is synced to an online account, such as Google, the copy is synced too. Choose Hide client names to show each booking only as Booked.

Backups, reports and exports

These are made only when you ask, and go only where you choose:

  • Backup file. Everything, photos included, except your PIN and this phone's backup and calendar settings, compressed and encrypted with AES-256 using a key made from your backup password. Without the password it cannot be opened, by anyone.
  • Daily backup. The same file, saved to the one folder you picked, once a day when something has changed. The newest seven are kept and older ones deleted. If that folder is synced to a cloud service, that service's own privacy terms apply to the copy.
  • Takings report. A PDF of the period you choose. It lists payments with clients' names, so keep it safe.
  • Spreadsheets. CSV files of takings, bookings or clients. These are not encrypted.
  • A client's record. A file of everything held about one client, for you to give them when they ask.

When you share a file, Android's share sheet passes it to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy terms apply.

Widgets and notifications

The Today and Next widgets show client names, times and services on your home screen, so anyone holding your unlocked phone can see them. Leave them off if that does not suit you. The quick actions widget shows no data. Notifications never show client names.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays until you delete it. You can delete any booking, client, photo, formula or block, forget a client and everything about them in one step, or remove clients not seen for a number of years. Uninstalling MChair deletes everything the app holds. Backup files, reports and spreadsheets saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Rights

Because the developer holds no data about you or your clients, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. If a client asks you for their data, or asks you to delete it, MChair's Data protection tools let you do that on your phone.

Security

The data file and photos are encrypted with a key that never leaves Android's Keystore. The app lock uses a PIN of four to six digits, stored only as a PBKDF2 hash, and pauses after five wrong tries. Android's own app backup is switched off for MChair, so your clients are not copied to a Google account without your knowledge. No method is perfect: keep your phone locked and your backup password private.
