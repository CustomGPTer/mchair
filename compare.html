What the others do well

Fresha, Booksy and Timely let clients book online at any hour, take card payments and deposits, send marketing and keep the diary in step across every device in the salon. Fresha and Booksy also list your salon on a marketplace where new clients can find you. MChair does none of those things. It is a private diary on one phone, for a hairdresser who takes their own bookings and would rather not pay every month.
