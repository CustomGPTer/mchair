What MChair does not do

MChair has no online booking: clients cannot book themselves, and reminders are sent from your own phone by text or WhatsApp. It does not take card payments itself; it records how you were paid. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site and install new versions over the old one.

Where the others do more

Booksy Biz and Fresha let clients book themselves online through your booking page, Instagram, Google and Facebook, and help new clients find you. Fresha includes a full point-of-sale tool. Both are on Google Play and update themselves.
