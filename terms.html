What MChair is

MChair is an appointment diary and record-keeping tool for mobile hairdressers. It helps you book, plan travel and time off, keep client records and see your takings and miles. It is not an online booking service, a payment service, a sat nav, an accounting or tax system, or professional advice of any kind.

Skin tests and client safety

Skin test reminders and warnings are there to help you follow your own procedures and your product manufacturers' instructions. They are only as good as the records and settings you enter, and they may not appear every time they could help. You remain responsible for carrying out skin tests, following manufacturers' instructions and deciding whether a service is safe for a client.

Your clients' data

You are responsible for the details you keep about your clients, including telling them how you use their details and keeping only what you need, under UK data protection law. MChair gives you tools to help, but using them is up to you.

Only send texts and WhatsApp messages to clients who expect to hear from you. Automatic texts are sent from your phone at your phone network's normal rates.

Travel and safety

Travel times and miles come from what you enter. Real journeys can take longer, so leave yourself time, and always drive safely: do not use the app while driving. Sending your day to someone is there to help, but it is not a lone-worker alarm and nobody is alerted if you do not arrive.

Takings, mileage and reports

Takings, mileage, reports and spreadsheets are summaries of what you entered. The mileage claim uses the rates set in the app, which you should check against HMRC's published rates each tax year. Check everything before relying on it for tax returns or accounts. None of it replaces your own records or advice from an accountant.

Reminders and widgets

Reminders and automatic texts may arrive late, or not at all, if Android holds them back, the phone is off, or there is no signal. Do not rely on MChair alone for anything time-critical.

Your data and backups

Your data is kept only on your phone. If you lose or reset the phone, forget your PIN or uninstall the app without a backup, your diary cannot be recovered by anyone. Keeping backups, and remembering your backup password, is your responsibility.
