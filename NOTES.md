The first release of MChair, a free appointment diary for mobile hairdressers on Android.

- Visits out, clients at your home and venues such as care homes, in one diary, each in its own colour.
- Travel linked up across the day: from home, from visit to visit, and home again, blocked out so you're never booked where you can't get to.
- Out days and home days, with free times that only show what fits.
- The day's route in Google Maps, and your day sent to someone you trust.
- Mileage with the claim at HMRC's simplified rates, and call-out fees.
- Everything from Chair: reminders, client cards, colour formulas, skin tests, photos, repeat bookings, waiting list, takings with a PDF report, widgets and password-locked backups. Chair backups open in MChair.
- No adverts, no account, no monthly fee. It cannot go online.

Android 8 or later. Full notes: https://customgpter.github.io/mchair/releases.html
